What every invoice was checked for (16 checks)
  • Not fully read extraction: the extractor recognised the layout and read every item row
  • Details missing required_fields: vendor, invoice number, invoice date, at least one line item and a total are present
  • Line incomplete line_incomplete: every line item has an amount, and a quantity and unit price either both or neither
  • Line total is wrong line_math: quantity x unit price = line amount, within a cent (when both are printed)
  • Items don't add up lines_sum: the line amounts add up to the subtotal, within a cent (to total minus tax when no subtotal is printed)
  • Tax doesn't match its rate tax_rate: when a tax rate is printed, tax = rate x subtotal (a cent of rounding allowed per two lines)
  • Total is wrong total_math: subtotal + tax = total, within a cent
  • Zero or negative total total_not_positive: the total is above zero (credit notes and refunds are left to a person)
  • Date in the future date_future: the invoice date is not after the run date
  • Very old invoice date_stale: the invoice date is not older than --max-age-days (365 by default; 0 turns this off)
  • Due date unreadable date_unreadable: a printed due date can be read as a date
  • Due before it was issued due_before_issue: the due date is not before the invoice date
  • Sent twice duplicate_number: no other file in the batch has the same vendor and invoice number
  • May be a duplicate possible_duplicate: no other file has the same vendor, date and total under a different invoice number, or the same invoice number with the same total or date under a different vendor name
  • Seen in an earlier run already_seen: the vendor and invoice number are not in the --history file of earlier runs
  • Number not on the page not_in_source: (AI extraction only) every amount and the invoice number appear in the source document
Only PASS invoices are in import_ready.csv. Every other file is listed above with its reason.
